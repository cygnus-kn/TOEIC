### Part 2: Respond to an Email

Question 6

From: Golden Smith bookshop [golden_smith_customer@goldensmith.com]
To: Regular customers SUBJECT: Joyful Reader
Sent: Dec 1st, 13:10

Dear Loyal Customer,
Thanks for your purchase at Golden Smith bookshop. We hope that all the books bought and any customized services received at our store were satisfactory. From the current year, when customers turn in their completed registration cards, the date of purchase and personal information will be automatically recorded and used to sign up for a membership in our Joyful Reader program. If you have further inquires about membership, please let us know by phone, fax, letter or e-mail. Thank you for shopping with us.

Direction: Respond to the e-mail as if you are a customer of the Golden Smith bookshop. In your e-mail, describe ONE problem that you have experienced so far and make TWO requests for information about the Joyful Reader program.

Question 7

From: Pam Hadis
To: James Carter, Human Resources Department
Subject: Job request
Sent: October 10, 9:53 A.M.

Dear Mr. Carter,
My name is Pam Hadis, and I am very much interested in applying for a job at Golden Tree Enterprises. I understand that your company usually posts job openings at the end of the year, but I would really appreciate it if you would send me information about upcoming jobs before making it public. I am available for an interview at any time. An early reply would be greatly appreciated.

Sincerely,
Pam Hadis

Direction: Respond as if you work for Golden Tree Enterprises where you are the director of the Human Resources Department. In your e-mail, tell her at least ONE reason why you refuse to comply with her request and give TWO pieces of information.
